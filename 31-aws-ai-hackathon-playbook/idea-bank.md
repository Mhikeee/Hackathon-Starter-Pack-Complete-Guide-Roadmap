# Idea Bank: 10 Shells You Can Adapt in 20 Minutes

You don't know the challenge yet, but you can guess the neighborhood. Two things point to it:

- **The event theme:** *AI, Careers, and the Evolving World of Work.*
- **What RELX does:** Elsevier (research papers, ScienceDirect, Scopus, medical and nursing education, clinical references), LexisNexis Legal (law, contracts), LexisNexis Risk Solutions (fraud and identity), and RX (events and exhibitions). REPH's Philippine teams work on content, data, AI, and technology for these businesses.

Each shell below is a ready-made app design. On Friday you **bend the closest shell to fit the real challenge**, which is much faster than inventing from zero. Every shell can be built in PartyRock (see [aws-no-code-guide.md](aws-no-code-guide.md)).

## The 20-minute adapt method (12:30–12:50)

| Minutes | Step |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Underline the **user** and the **pain** in the challenge statement. Write: "The user is ___. Their problem is ___. Success looks like ___." |
| 5–10 | Everyone silently picks the 1–2 closest shells (or a fresh idea). Change the user and pain to match the challenge. Add a **twist** (Filipino/Taglish, offline-friendly, for a group others ignore, one extra step no one else does). |
| 10–17 | Score the 2–3 candidates with the 20-minute problem-picking rubric in the [section README](README.md). |
| 17–20 | Write the one-liner: **"For [user] who [pain], [App name] is an AI [thing] that [result], unlike [how they do it today]."** If you can't write it, the idea isn't ready. |

## Careers and the future of work (the event theme)

### 1. SkillBridge: job-gap coach
- **User:** 4th-year student or fresh grad applying for their first tech/business job.
- **Pain:** They don't know which skills a job post actually wants or what to learn first.
- **Widgets:** Input `Resume` + Input `Job Post` → Text `Gap Analysis` (table: skill · have it? · evidence) → Text `2-Week Learning Plan` (free resources, daily tasks) → Chatbot `Mock Interviewer` (asks 3 questions about the gaps).
- **Wow moment:** Paste a real job post, and in seconds get a colored ✅/⚠️ skill table and a day-by-day plan.
- **Impact number:** Hours of confused research saved per application. PH youth unemployment/underemployment data (look it up if Wi-Fi allows; otherwise speak qualitatively).

### 2. Interview Dojo: practice interviewer with feedback
- **User:** Students with no one to practice interviews with, especially first-gen job seekers.
- **Pain:** Nervousness and unstructured answers.
- **Widgets:** Input `Role` + Input `Your Answer` → Chatbot `Interviewer` (asks one question at a time) → Text `Feedback` (STAR method score 1–5, a better version of the answer, one tip).
- **Wow moment:** Answer badly on purpose live, then show the rewrite.
- **Impact:** Practice anytime, free. Tie it to REPH's own hiring (they're hiring interns).

### 3. Automate-or-Upskill: future-of-work check for employees
- **User:** An office worker (or a REPH team member) worried about AI replacing tasks.
- **Pain:** They don't know which of their tasks AI will change, or what to learn.
- **Widgets:** Input `My weekly tasks` → Text `Task breakdown` (table: task · AI can help? · how · time saved) → Text `Upskilling path` (3 skills to grow, why) → Image `My future role` card.
- **Wow moment:** A list of boring tasks turns into "you'd save 6 hrs/week, here's what to learn."
- **Impact:** Hours per week, and it reframes AI as a helper instead of a threat (a great fit for the theme).

### 4. Onboarding Buddy: new-hire assistant
- **User:** New employee in their first 2 weeks.
- **Pain:** Too many documents; they're afraid to ask "dumb questions."
- **Widgets:** Document `Employee handbook` → Chatbot `Buddy` (answers only from the document, says "Ask HR" if unsure) + Text `My first-week checklist`.
- **Wow moment:** Ask "what do I do if I'm sick?" and get the exact rule quoted.
- **Impact:** HR questions reduced; faster time-to-productivity. Bedrock Knowledge Bases is the "how it scales" story.

## Research, health, and education (Elsevier's world)

### 5. PaperPal PH: research papers in plain language
- **User:** Students, teachers, LGU/policy staff, and journalists who can't read dense research.
- **Pain:** Research is locked behind jargon (and English-only).
- **Widgets:** Document or Input `Abstract` → Text `Plain summary` (grade-8 level, 5 bullets) → Text `Taglish version` → Text `What this means for the Philippines` → Chatbot `Ask the paper`.
- **Wow moment:** Paste a hard abstract and get a 5-bullet Taglish explanation.
- **Impact:** Access to science for non-experts; supports research literacy.

### 6. Board-Exam Buddy: nursing/med review coach
- **User:** Nursing/medtech students reviewing for PH licensure exams (very large population).
- **Pain:** Expensive review centers, passive reading.
- **Widgets:** Input `Topic` + Input `Level` → Text `10-question quiz` (with answer key hidden in a second widget) → Text `Explanations` → Chatbot `Tutor`.
- **Wow moment:** Generate a quiz on "fluid & electrolytes" and get a tutor explanation for a wrong answer.
- **Impact:** Free practice for thousands of reviewees. Add a disclaimer: study aid, not medical advice.

## Legal and risk (LexisNexis's world)

### 7. ScamShield: text/email scam checker
- **User:** Filipinos receiving SMS/Messenger/email scams (job scams, fake deliveries, "you won").
- **Pain:** People lose money, and fake job offers target students and job seekers directly (links back to the careers theme).
- **Widgets:** Input `Paste the message` → Text `Risk rating` (🟢/🟡/🔴 + %) → Text `Red flags found` (bullets) → Text `What to do now` (report to CICC hotline 1326, block, don't click).
- **Wow moment:** Paste a real-looking fake job offer and it flags "asks for a placement fee; gmail address for a big company."
- **Impact:** Scam losses are huge and visible in the news; everyone in the room has received one.

### 8. ContractClear: employment contract explainer
- **User:** First-time employees signing contracts they don't understand.
- **Pain:** Hidden clauses (bond, non-compete, probation terms).
- **Widgets:** Document/Input `Contract` → Text `Plain summary` → Text `Watch-outs` (table: clause · what it means · question to ask HR) → Chatbot `Ask about my contract`.
- **Wow moment:** A 3-page contract becomes "⚠️ 2-year training bond: ₱100k penalty if you leave early."
- **Impact:** Protects new workers. Disclaimer: not legal advice; it helps you ask better questions.

## Business operations (the "real-world business challenge" angle)

### 9. FairPost: inclusive, skills-based job post writer
- **User:** HR / recruiters (like REPH's Talent Acquisition team).
- **Pain:** Job posts with inflated requirements scare off good candidates.
- **Widgets:** Input `Draft job post` → Text `Issues found` (biased words, unnecessary degree requirements, jargon) → Text `Improved post` → Text `Interview questions matching the real skills`.
- **Wow moment:** A wordy, intimidating post becomes clear and welcoming, with an explanation of why.
- **Impact:** Bigger, more diverse applicant pools. Pairs nicely with SkillBridge as the "other side" of the market.

### 10. EventMatch: networking matchmaker for events
- **User:** Attendees at conferences, job fairs, and exhibitions (RX's business).
- **Pain:** People attend but don't meet the right people.
- **Widgets:** Input `About me + goals` + Document `Attendee/booth list` → Text `Top 5 people/booths to meet and why` → Text `Icebreaker for each`.
- **Wow moment:** Use today's event as the demo: "here's who I should talk to at the AI Summit."
- **Impact:** Better event ROI for attendees and organizers.

## Innovation twists (add one to any shell)

- **Language:** Filipino/Taglish/Bisaya output.
- **Underserved user:** PWDs, provincial students, OFW families, senior citizens.
- **Two-sided:** Serve both sides of a problem (job seeker + recruiter).
- **Action, not answer:** End with a concrete plan, message draft, or checklist the user can use today.
- **Trust layer:** Show *why* (sources, red flags, confidence level), not only *what*.
- **Human-in-the-loop:** The AI drafts, a human approves. Say this out loud; judges from a real company care about safety.

## Traps to avoid

- A general "ask me anything" chatbot. It has zero innovation points.
- Ideas that need real private data you don't have (medical records, bank data). Use realistic *sample* data and say so.
- Promising accuracy you can't show. Demo it, or don't claim it.
- Picking the idea you love when it doesn't answer the challenge.
